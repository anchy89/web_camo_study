Camouflage Detection Study

Measuring how quickly people detect camouflaged objects in forest scenes

🎯 Find the object hidden in each scene - click where you think it is, or press Space if you think there's none.
🖥️ Takes about 5 minutes. Requires a computer with a mouse. Do not use a trackpad if possible.

Your responses are anonymous and will only be used for research purposes. Cursor movement, click/space button press and timing are recorded. Participation is voluntary and can be stopped at any time.
